PASSPORT WEEK (Fall 2025)

Unlock Boundless Horizons with Your Passport!

A passport is very important if you want to study or travel abroad. It is the first thing you need when applying for international studies or foreign scholarships. Without a passport, it is not possible to apply for these opportunities.

To help students get started, we are organizing ‘Passport Week’. During this week, we will guide you through the online passport application process and help you with the required documents. So, book your slot, bring your documents, and come join us during Passport Week, where our volunteers will guide you to apply. Let’s get your passport ready and open the door to exciting opportunities around the world!
